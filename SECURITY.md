# Security Policy

The package performs pure local arithmetic and has no network, filesystem, shell, credential, or broker integration surface.

Broker operations are untrusted input. Consumers must preserve immutable source evidence and independently reconcile quantities, currencies, fees, corporate actions, transfers, corrections, and opening positions before relying on analytics. Partial coverage and quality warnings must not be suppressed.

Report vulnerabilities through https://github.com/FrankFMY without attaching real account operations or personal data.
